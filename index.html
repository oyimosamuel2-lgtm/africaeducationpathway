Who we are

About Us

AED African Education Pathway is a leading educational consultancy firm dedicated to empowering African students to achieve their academic dreams abroad. We provide comprehensive guidance and support to students aspiring to study in prestigious institutions across the globe, particularly in the USA, Canada and the UK.

Navigating the international education landscape can be complex and overwhelming. Our experienced team of education consultants acts as a bridge — simplifying the process and ensuring a smooth transition for every student.

We believe in personalised guidance, recognising that each student's aspirations, academic background and financial circumstances are unique.

Vision

To be the premier educational consultancy in Africa, renowned for its commitment to excellence, integrity, and transformative impact on students' lives.

Mission

To provide comprehensive and personalised educational consulting services that empower African students to access quality international education, enabling them to achieve their full potential and contribute meaningfully to their communities and the world.

Motto

“Empowering Dreams, Building Futures.”

Connecting talented African minds with world‑class educational opportunities.

What we do

Our Services

End‑to‑end support, from your first consultation to your first semester abroad.

University Selection

We help you identify universities that align with your academic goals, programme interests and financial capabilities.

Application Assistance

We guide you through the entire application — compelling personal statements, recommendation letters and all required documents.

Financial Aid & Scholarships

We share information on available scholarships, grants and financial aid options, and help you apply for them.

Visa Processing Support

We explain visa requirements and support you step by step through the visa application process.

Pre‑Departure Orientation

We prepare you for life abroad — travel, accommodation, academic expectations and cultural adjustment — before you fly.

Post‑Arrival Support

We help you settle in by connecting you with alumni networks and practical resources in your new country.

Standardised Test Preparation

Coaching and resources for TOEFL, IELTS, SAT, GRE, GMAT and more.
